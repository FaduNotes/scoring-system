📋 RULES & VIOLATIONS

Pencak Silat Competition Regulation FEB-2026

1. LIGHT VIOLATIONS - Pelanggaran Ringan

Hukuman: Pembinaan → Teguran → -1 Point

Examples:
- Kaidah Pencak Silat ka istemal na karna
- Gelanggang se bahar nikalna
- Bahar waalon se baat karna
- 10 sec se zyada passive rehna
- Jaan bujhkar peeth dikhana
- Dono haath bandh ke pasang karna
- Wasit ki baat na manna
- Sapuan/Guntingan maarna par opponent na girna
- Striking range mein neeche pasang karna
- Face ki taraf 5 ungli dikhana - feinting
- Pair matras pe hai aur seedha haath se pakadna

2. MODERATE VIOLATIONS - Pelanggaran Sedang

Hukuman: Teguran langsung

Examples:
- Body protector/kapda pakad ke punch/kick marna
- Gummul karna, baal ya kerudung kheenchna
- Neeche gire opponent ko pair se dabana
- Jatuhan ke baad bhi marna
- Body protector + upper baju pakad ke jatuhan
- Bina attack ke seedha kheenchna
- Bahut door se sapuan/guntingan
- Jaan bujhkar bahar dhakelna
- Invalid target pe grazing attack
- Valid se invalid target pe shift karna
- Counter-attack jatuhan
- Time waste karna - dheere chalna, gear theek karna
- 3 min se zyada kapda change karna
- Gong ke baad marna
- Wasit ke peeche chupna
- Wasit ko chhuna
- Diving - invalid target dikhana
- Defense mein opponent ko chipakna

3. SERIOUS VIOLATIONS - Pelanggaran Berat

Hukuman: Peringatan I, II, III → Disqualification

Examples:
- Head, neck, kemaluan pe attack - injury ho ya na ho
- Wasit ko dhakelna/kheenchna
- Knee joint pe direct attack
- Single attack to thigh bina awalan/akhiran
- Bahar dhakel ke injury karna
- Headbutt ya head se attack
- Invalid target pe attack se injury
- MULAI se pehle ya BERHENTI ke baad maar ke injury
- Kaatna aur kharonchna
- Kapda pakad ke elbow/knee marna
- Gaali dena, thookna, provoke karna
- Pile driving - gardan pe giraana
- Seedha pakad ke giraana

4. DISQUALIFICATION - Diskualifikasi

Kab hoti hai:
- Peringatan II ke baad Peringatan III milna
- Jaan bujhkar serious violation - sportsmanship ke khilaaf
- Opponent injured aur khel nahi sakta - doctor confirm kare
- Jaan bujhkar opponent ka sar matras pe marna
- 3rd call ke baad bhi arena mein na aana

Note: Disqualification sirf Wasit declare kar sakta hai
